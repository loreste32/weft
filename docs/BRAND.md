## Why “Weft”

In weaving, the **weft** is the thread shot through the warp — it binds the cloth together.  
Weft scripts weave **models, tools, files, HTTP, and concurrency** into short programs you can run and train models on.

## Mascot: Wifty

**Wifty** is a cheerful indigo weaver bird with a loop of gold thread.  
Small, quick, and good at tying things together — like a Weft script.

![Wifty](../assets/brand/wifty.jpg)

Use Wifty on docs, stickers, READMEs, and social previews.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Prefer show over tell.  
- Friendly, not corporate. Technical without jargon walls.  
- Never claim to be “Python but faster” as the whole story — Weft is its own language for LLM work.
